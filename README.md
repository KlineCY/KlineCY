## Research

### CTA Multi-Asset Long–Short Strategy

Built a systematic research framework across 37 ETFs in six asset classes: U.S. broad-market equities, U.S. sector equities, international equities, fixed income, commodities, and alternative assets. The project combines six groups of features—including momentum, trend, and volatility—with monthly rolling training and evaluation of seven predictive models. Ridge was selected using validation results; portfolio construction uses Ledoit–Wolf covariance estimation and constrained optimization with risk, turnover, and exposure considerations.

### DICE Model Research

Authored a research paper through the Jinglan Research Program at Fudan University School of Economics on the development and contemporary relevance of the DICE model, examining its economic logic, applications, and limitations.
